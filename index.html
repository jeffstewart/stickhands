Getting Started with Stickhands

⚡

1. Play on Keyboard

No drum kit connected? Play immediately right on your computer keyboard:

A Kick S Snare D Hi-Hat F Open Hat G Tom 1 H Tom 2 J Floor Tom K Crash L Ride

Hold Shift for louder velocity hits. Press Space to play or pause.

🥁

2. Electronic Drum Kit (Web MIDI)

Plug in your Roland, Alesis, Yamaha, or any e-kit via USB:

  • Supported in Chromium browsers (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera).
  • Click Connect MIDI in the top bar to allow browser access and link your kit.
  • Once connected, your pads are live and ready to play!
  • Pro Tip: Want to hear your drum module's internal sounds? Set Settings → Pad sounds → Off. Run your computer's audio into your drum module's Aux-In to hear the metronome, count-in, and backing band alongside your kit!
🥢

3. Hands-Free Stick Navigation

Never put your sticks down to reach for the mouse! When paused or finished:

Crash: Up
Kick: Down
Tom 1: Left
Tom 2: Right
Floor Tom: Select / Enter
Ride: Back

Destructive actions (like deleting songs) are protected mouse-only.

📚

4. 21 Lessons & Custom Songs

Ready to practice out of the box:

  • 21 Progressive Lessons: Click Songs for structured tracks covering Basics, Grooves, Fills, Intermediate, and Capstone.
  • Practice Looping: Click Loop to isolate and drill difficult bars at any tempo.
  • Import Your Own Charts: Click Songs → Manage library → Add track to import any .mid / .midi or uncompressed .musicxml file!
